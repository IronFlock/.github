# Security policy

This policy covers every repository of the IronFlock organisation and the
products built from them: the IronFlock cloud platform, the IronFlock
appliance, the device agent and device images, the SDKs and the dashboard
widgets.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately, not in public issues, pull requests
or discussions.

- **Email:** [security@ironflock.com](mailto:security@ironflock.com)
- **GitHub:** where a repository shows *Report a vulnerability* under its
  *Security* tab, you can use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting instead.

Please include what you can of:

- the affected product, component and version (or commit),
- the steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept,
- the impact you expect, and whether you know of active exploitation,
- how we can reach you, and whether and how you want to be credited.

## What happens next

- We confirm that we received your report within 5 working days.
- We assess it, keep you informed about our progress, and tell you when a fix
  is available.
- We agree a disclosure date with you. Once the fix is available, we publish
  an advisory and credit you if you wish.

## Testing guidelines

When you look for vulnerabilities, please:

- use your own accounts, devices and data only, and never access, change or
  delete data of other users;
- avoid anything that degrades the service for others (no denial-of-service or
  load testing against the cloud platform);
- stop and report as soon as you have found a vulnerability, and do not use it
  further;
- give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you publish anything.

We will not pursue legal action against research that follows these
guidelines in good faith.
